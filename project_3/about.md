# BusinessInsights Hub - Unified Analytics Platform

A business intelligence dashboard that consolidates data from multiple sources (sales, customers, inventory, finance) into a single interactive interface. Built to help businesses track KPIs, analyze revenue trends, segment customers, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Data Pipeline: Apache Airflow, dbt, Apache Spark**
- **Backend: Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy**
- **Database: PostgreSQL, Redis**
- **Frontend: React (Next.js), Recharts, Tailwind CSS**
- **Cloud & DevOps: AWS S3, Docker, GitHub Actions**

---

## 🔑 Key Features

### 1. Unified KPI & Revenue Analytics
* Real-time tracking for revenue, profit margins, and active customers
* Interactive monthly and yearly revenue trend comparisons
* Category-wise sales distribution and visual breakdowns

### 2. Customer Segmentation & Analytics
* Automated customer classification using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis
* Behavioral tracking across sales channels
* Retention and churn risk metrics visualization

### 3. Inventory & Product Performance Tracking
* Top-performing product rankings by sales volume and margin
* Low-stock and reorder point notifications
* Cross-category stock turn rate analysis

### 4. Interactive Reporting & Exporting
* Dynamic date range and multi-region filtering
* Customized scheduled reports exportable to PDF and Excel
* Low-latency dashboard view caching powered by Redis
